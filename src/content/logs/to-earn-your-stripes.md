The tiger felt lighter, leaving the now-dark-orange stream.

With visible disgust, the other young tigers, covered in bountiful black stripes, looked at the old tiger’s peltless, stripeless, naked, wrinkly face, wishing for a kinder future.

The bald tiger silently wished for their best. That they catch the uncatchable, dream beyond their small patch of jungle. Beyond Mowgli. That they catch the prey they were put here to find, and roar past the adverse vines blocking their path.

The tiger felt lighter. He looked back to the stream and caught his reflection. He smiled, seeing his real stripes.

*Written December 27, 2019. Edited October 30, 2026.*
